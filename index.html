About Radio DADAA

Radio DADAA is an online radio station that covers artistic and alternative stories from a disability perspective. From genre-skipping local music and powerful audio dramas, to storytelling with depth and conversations that truly matter, Radio DADAA is a creative platform for authentic voices.

Got Something to Say?

We are always on the lookout for bold voices, big ideas, and local legends to join our community of content creators and presenters:

  • Host your own show
  • Create content that connects
  • Share stories from your community
  • Explore experimental audio
  • Full training, support, and studio time provided
Contact radioinfo@dadaa.org.au
